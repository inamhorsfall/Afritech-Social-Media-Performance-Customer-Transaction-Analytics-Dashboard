## 💡 Key Insights

- **Negative sentiment** accounts for 40.87% of all brand mentions (1,107M), while positive sentiment holds at 18.25% — highlighting a significant reputation management challenge.
- **53.12% of crises remain unresolved** (263M), with only 46.88% successfully resolved, indicating a need for faster crisis response protocols.
- **TikTok** drives the highest engagement rate across all platforms, outperforming Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, and Facebook combined.
- **VIP customers** (67 out of 200) generate a disproportionately high share of revenue, making retention of this segment critical to business performance.
- **$28.90M revenue is at risk** — nearly 50% of total revenue — driven by product recall impact and declining transaction volumes in Q4.
- **Returning customers** contribute 42.14% of revenue ($24.25M), while New customers account for 48.54% ($27.93M), suggesting strong acquisition but a retention gap.
- **Laptop and Smartphone** are the top-performing product categories by brand mention and transaction volume, while Smartwatch lags significantly.
- **Product recall** affects 49.77% of products (28.63M units), with recalled products showing sharply reduced transaction volumes across all categories.

## 🗂️ Dashboard Features

### Social Media Page
- **Engagement Rate by Platform** — horizontal bar chart across TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook
- **Sentiment Distribution** — donut chart (Positive 18.25% / Neutral 40.87% / Negative 40.87%)
- **Brand vs Competitor Mention** — grouped bar chart by platform
- **Engagement Trends Over Time** — multi-line chart (Likes, Shares, Comments) Jan 2023 – Oct 2023
- **Crisis Trends Over Time** — volume trend Jan 2022 – Jul 2023
- **Crisis Resolution** — donut chart (Resolved 46.88% vs Unresolved 53.12%)
- **Filters** — Social Media type, Customer Profile, Transaction & date range (Nov 2022 – Nov 2023)

### Customer Profile Page
- **Customers by Region** — bar chart across 40+ US states and regions
- **Transaction Volume by Product & Customer Type** — stacked bar (New / Returning / VIP) across Laptop, Smartphone, Tablet, Smartwatch
- **Customer Distribution** — donut (New 11.5% / VIP 35.5% / Returning 53.5%)
- **Customer Type Distribution by Revenue** — donut showing revenue share per segment
- **Filters** — Age, Customer Type (New / Returning / VIP)

### Transaction & Product Page
- **Revenue Trend** — monthly line chart ($4M–$5M range)
- **Transactions by Month** — bar chart showing seasonality peaks
- **Revenue by Product Category** — Laptop ($25M), Smartphone ($14M), Tablet ($11M), Smartwatch ($8M)
- **Recall Impact by Product** — grouped bar (Recalled True vs False) per category
- **Revenue at Risk Over Time** — area/bar combo chart by quarter (2021–2023)
- **Product Recalled** — donut (Recalled 49.77% vs Not Recalled 50.23%)
- **Filters** — Product Recalled (True/False), Transaction Date (2021/2022/2023)
